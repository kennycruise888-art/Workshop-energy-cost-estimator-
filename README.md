# Workshop-energy-cost-estimator-
 Workshop Energy Cost Estimator

The Workshop Energy Cost Estimator is a software application designed to calculate and estimate the energy consumption and operating cost of machines, tools, and electrical equipment used in a workshop. The system helps workshop owners, technicians, engineers, and students monitor electricity usage, estimate expenses, and identify opportunities to reduce energy costs.

The estimator works by collecting information such as the power rating of each machine (in watts or kilowatts), the number of hours it operates, the quantity of equipment in use, and the local electricity tariff. Using standard electrical energy formulas, the system calculates the total energy consumed in kilowatt-hours (kWh) and determines the corresponding electricity cost over a selected period, such as daily, weekly, or monthly.

The project provides an easy-to-use interface where users can enter machine details, view estimated energy costs, and generate reports. It can also compare the energy usage of different machines, helping users identify equipment that consumes excessive power. This information supports better energy management, cost control, maintenance planning, and efficient allocation of workshop resources.

Overall, the Workshop Energy Cost Estimator promotes energy efficiency, reduces unnecessary electricity expenses, and assists users in making informed decisions about equipment operation. It is particularly useful for engineering workshops, manufacturing industries, technical institutions, and small businesses that depend on electrical machinery for daily operations.
